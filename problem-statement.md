
### 1. Support-Ticket Triage Agent
**Problem:** Incoming support tickets (public dataset or your own inbox) need classification, related-issue retrieval, draft response generation, and escalation of ambiguous cases to a human.
**Build:** Planner/executor split, tool calling against a real ticketing API or GitHub Issues API, confidence-based escalation threshold, full audit trail of every decision.
**Signal:** Founding-engineer-style ownership of a real business process end-to-end.
